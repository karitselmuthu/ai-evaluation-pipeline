# General Knowledge Fact Sheet

## 1. Science and Biology
Mitochondria are membrane-bound cell organelles that generate most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell, and are known as the powerhouse of the cell.
Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to perform photosynthesis.
Oxygen is represented by the chemical symbol O and has an atomic number of 8.
White blood cells, or leukocytes, play a key role in defending the body against pathogens and infections.
The Sun is classified as a star at the center of the Solar System, not a planet.
Many birds fly, but species like penguins and ostriches are flightless.

## 2. Geography & Space
The Pacific Ocean is situated on the west coast of the United States.
Greenland is recognized as the world's largest island that is not a continent.
The Grand Canyon is located in Arizona, and Phoenix is the capital of Arizona.
The Great Pyramid of Giza is located in Egypt. The official currency of Egypt is the Egyptian Pound.
The Apollo 11 lunar module landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969.
No humans have colonised Mars yet. Exploratory missions are planned for the late 2030s.

## 3. Math & Science Rules
The square root of 144 is 12.
1 kilometer is equal to 1,000 meters.
15 multiplied by 8 equals 120.
The perimeter of a rectangle is calculated as 2 * (length + width).
At standard atmospheric pressure (sea level), water boils at 100 degrees Celsius.

## 4. Pop Culture, Movies & Literature
"My Heart Will Go On" is the main theme song for the 1997 movie Titanic. Titanic was directed by James Cameron, starring Leonardo DiCaprio. Leonardo DiCaprio was nominated for several awards for Titanic, but he did not win an Academy Award for this performance.
To Kill a Mockingbird is a novel by Harper Lee, published in 1960.
Ishmael is the narrator and main protagonist of Herman Melville's novel Moby-Dick.
Paris is the capital of France. The Eiffel Tower is a famous iron tower located in Paris.

## 5. History & Timeline
The Treaty of Versailles was signed in 1919. World War II began in 1939.
Alice was born on May 14, 2010. The current year is 2026. The minimum age for a driver's license is 18.

## 6. Technology & Acronyms
NASA stands for National Aeronautics and Space Administration.
CPU stands for Central Processing Unit.
Apple announced its new M3 chip during the keynote event. Tim Cook is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Apple Inc.

## 7. How-To Guides
To start the engine: Step 1: Insert key into the ignition. Step 2: Press down the brake pedal firmly. Step 3: Turn the key clockwise.
To brew tea: First, boil water. Next, pour the water over the teabag in a mug. Finally, let it steep for 3-5 minutes.
